03 · Process

How I test

The general process behind every report above, from first recon to disclosure.

01

Recon & mapping

Enumerate subdomains, endpoints and parameters before touching anything — understand the attack surface first.

02

Access & auth review

Check who can reach what: broken access control, session handling, and 2FA/password-reset flows.

03

Business logic

Look past the obvious checklist for flaws in how the application is actually supposed to behave.

04

Exploit & document

Build a minimal, reproducible proof of concept with clear steps, requests, and impact — no guesswork for the triager.

05

Responsible disclosure

Report through the program's official channel only, and stay within defined scope at every step.
